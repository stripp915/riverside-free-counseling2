About us

Help for those who need it, at no cost.

The River Valley Free Counseling Clinic serves the people in Russellville, Pope County and surrounding counties who are in need of mental health services but are without insurance coverage and are unable to afford care.

Our compassionate mental health professionals volunteer their time to help those in need so that there is no fee, no co-pay and no billing of insurance at all. Our services are completely free for those who meet our income and insurance guidelines.

Services provided

A broad range of counseling for life’s challenges.

We offer counseling to assist those struggling with:

  • Depression
  • Anxiety
  • Family issues
  • Marriage issues
  • Significant other relationships
  • Parenting
  • Conflict resolution
  • Anger management
  • Addictions recovery
  • Grief
  • Childhood trauma
  • Adult trauma
  • Self-esteem
  • Spiritual questions
  • Quality of life issues
  • And a variety of other life challenges
